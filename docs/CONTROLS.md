# Controls

You can use a keyboard, mouse, controller, or a combination. Android also has
touch controls. Open **Controls** in the launcher or in-game settings to change
your setup.

## Camera mod

With Modern Analogue Camera installed, open **Mods → Installed → Modern Analogue Camera → Details and settings** to remap look directions, recenter, zoom and first person for keyboard, mouse and controller. Mouse look is enabled by default, with its own sensitivity and recenter-button settings on the same page. Invert horizontal and vertical look separately in the mod options; both settings apply in third and first person. Press Escape to open settings and release the cursor. Bindings are saved on this device and take priority over matching N64 bindings while the mod controls the camera.

| Camera action | Keyboard | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Look | I / K / J / L | Right stick |
| Recenter | U | Right-stick click |
| Cycle zoom | O | Left-stick click |
| Toggle first person | P | Top face button |

Tap and release the mode buttons. Zoom cycles the three original distances, subject to the game's camera restrictions. First person keeps its original viewpoint and left-stick controls, with right-stick and mouse look available too. Recenter brings the camera behind Rocket at the current zoom level, with obstacle avoidance still active. In first person, it looks in the direction Rocket is facing. The middle mouse button recenters by default. Assigning it to another camera action removes that recenter shortcut.

The camera mod applies speed, response, deadzone, momentum and inversion changes immediately. **Camera momentum** controls input glide and the game's follow smoothing together. Set it to 0 for direct movement, or raise it for a softer start and stop. Obstacle checks still run at every setting. **Stick response** controls how quickly stick or keyboard look reaches full speed; 30 gives the quickest response. Fast mouse turns are limited to keep the original obstacle solver stable.

Use **Active in this game** to turn the running camera mod off or on. It also works after starting with the mod disabled, provided the included version was installed in your selected profile before launch. A separate **Enabled next launch** choice appears when you select a different profile or package version. The status below the mod shows when the switch has reached the game.

## Default keyboard controls

| N64 control | Keyboard |
| --- | --- |
| Analogue stick | W, A, S, D |
| A | Space or X |
| B | Z or Left Ctrl |
| Z trigger | Left or Right Shift |
| Start | Enter |
| D-pad | Arrow keys |
| L / R | Q / E |
| C-Up / C-Down / C-Left / C-Right | I / K / J / L |

F1 or Escape opens settings. F11 or Alt+Enter switches fullscreen.
The F1/F11 bindings can be changed in **Shortcuts**. Escape and Alt+Enter
remain available even if you change those bindings.

## Controller Studio

Open **N64 Controls**, then select a button on the controller diagram or choose
a group above it. The card shows the selected N64 control and its current inputs.
Click an input to replace it. **Extra inputs** opens the second keyboard and
controller slots.

The **Keyboard / mouse** slots accept mouse buttons, wheel directions and keys.
To assign mouse movement, tick **Capture mouse movement** in the input prompt,
then move clearly in one direction. During gameplay, movement bindings capture
the cursor; Escape opens settings and releases it. Direct camera mouse look
takes priority over movement bindings while the camera mod owns the view.

Release any held button before pressing the new input, and let the stick return
to centre before assigning an axis. Escape cancels the assignment. Backspace,
Delete or **Remove Input** clears it. Unplugging the selected controller or
switching away from the window also cancels an unfinished assignment.

If that input is already assigned elsewhere, choose **Swap Inputs**, **Share
Input**, or **Cancel**. Swap is available for a single conflicting assignment.
Changes save as you make them.

Controller face buttons are named by position: bottom, right, left and top.
This avoids confusion between controllers with different printed letters.
Choose the controller you want to use on the **Controller** tab.

## Guided Setup

Select **Guided Setup** if you'd rather work through each control in order.
The guide covers all 18 inputs, including the analogue directions, C buttons
and D-pad. Use Next and Back to move through them, then review your setup.
You can leave the guide at any time. Controls you skip keep their assignments.

## Testing and stick settings

**Test Inputs** highlights the N64 inputs your setup produces without passing
them to the game. Escape, controller Back/Select or **Done Testing** ends the test.
Leaving N64 Controls or closing the overlay also ends it.

Use the **Stick** tab to adjust deadzone, sensitivity, response and inversion.
Controller selection and Rumble Pak settings are on **Controller**.
Restoring N64 bindings does not reset those other settings.

## Android touch controls

The on-screen stick and buttons support simultaneous touches, so you can move
and jump together. Tap **Settings** or press Android Back to open the overlay.
Tap **Return to Game** or press Back again to continue playing.

**Hide Controls** removes the gameplay buttons when you're using a controller.
Settings and **Show Controls** stay on screen. The device's volume buttons
adjust media volume.

With Modern Analogue Camera active, a **LOOK** stick replaces the C-button cluster. Double tap it to recenter. **ZOOM** cycles the original camera distances; **VIEW** enters or leaves first person. The look stick works in both views.

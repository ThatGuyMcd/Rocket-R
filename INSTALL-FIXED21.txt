Rocket-R FIXED21 overlay
========================

Extract this ZIP directly into your existing D:\Rocket-R folder and overwrite
matching files. Do not delete build\private\rocket.us.z64 or your ROM.

Then run:
  D:\Rocket-R\ONE-CLICK-BUILD.cmd

FIXED21 intentionally regenerates stage 7. It keeps 0x80000400 as the initial
ROM load/DMA address, recompiles game_init (0x80000E64) as the callable host
entry, and recreates the useful retail bootstrap state (BSS clear + stack)
before game_init runs. This avoids the retail stub's intentional BREAK at
0x80000438 after game_init returns under N64ModernRuntime.
